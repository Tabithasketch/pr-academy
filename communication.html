Your Lessons 📚

Lesson 1: Understanding Communication

Communication is the process of sharing information, ideas, thoughts and feelings between people.

Elements of Communication

  • Sender: The person who sends a message.
  • Message: The information being shared.
  • Channel: The medium used to communicate.
  • Receiver: The person who receives the message.
  • Feedback: The receiver's response.

Why Communication Matters in PR

Public Relations professionals use communication to build trust, maintain relationships and share information with their publics.

Lesson 2: Verbal Communication

Verbal communication involves using spoken or written words to share information.

Examples

  • Speaking during a presentation.
  • Participating in a radio programme.
  • Writing a professional email.
  • Speaking during a meeting.

How to Improve

  • Speak clearly.
  • Use simple and appropriate words.
  • Organise your thoughts before speaking.
  • Practise speaking regularly.

Lesson 3: Non-verbal Communication

Non-verbal communication involves sending messages without using spoken or written words.

Examples

  • Eye contact: Looking at someone while communicating.
  • Facial expressions: Smiling, frowning or showing surprise.
  • Gestures: Using hand movements.
  • Posture: The way you sit or stand.
  • Body language: How your body communicates feelings and attitudes.

PR Application

A PR professional should maintain appropriate body language during interviews, meetings and presentations.

Lesson 4: Active Listening

Active listening means paying full attention to a speaker, understanding their message and responding appropriately.

Good Listening Habits

  • Give the speaker your attention.
  • Avoid interrupting.
  • Ask relevant questions.
  • Show interest through appropriate body language.
  • Give thoughtful feedback.

Why It Matters in PR

Listening helps PR professionals understand public opinions, identify concerns and build stronger relationships.
